MINNSACE INC. / WEBSITE INFORMATION

Website privacy

This notice applies to the Minnsace Inc. studio website at minnsace.com. It describes this website, not data handling within Deadlight Tavern or Steam.

A straightforward studio website

This site has no account registration, contact form, advertising, or analytics scripts. It does not set cookies or use browser storage. Brand images and styles are served with the website, rather than loaded from third-party font or media services.

Website hosting

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including visitors' IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for information about GitHub's handling of that data.

Contacting the studio

Email links open your email application; they do not submit information through this site. If you email hello@minnsace.com, Minnsace Inc. receives the address and information you choose to send, so we can respond to your enquiry. Please do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information.

Links to Steam

Game links take you to Steam, a separate service operated by Valve. Steam's own terms and privacy policy apply when you visit or use that service. This site does not embed Steam tracking, videos, or widgets.

Questions

For questions about this website, contact Minnsace Inc. at hello@minnsace.com.
