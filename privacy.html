MINNSACE INC. / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. This policy covers the Minnsace Inc. website at minnsace.com and Minnsace Inc. mobile apps, including Daily Verse. Minnsace Inc. is a game studio in Missouri, USA.

Our apps collect no personal data

Daily Verse and our other mobile apps do not require an account, and they do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. We run no analytics, advertising, or tracking services in our apps, and our apps contain no third-party tracking software.

What stays on your device

Game progress, streaks, statistics, and settings (such as your chosen Bible translation, difficulty, and reminder time) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to Minnsace Inc. The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. The Daily Verse widget reads game state stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Daily Verse offers an optional one-time in-app purchase, Daily Verse+ (Plus Unlock). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Minnsace Inc. never receives your payment card details or other payment information. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Information collected through this site

This site has no account registration, contact form, advertising, or analytics scripts. It does not set cookies or use browser storage. Brand images and styles are served with the website, rather than loaded from third-party font or media services.

Website hosting

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including visitors' IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for information about GitHub's handling of that data.

Contacting the studio

Email links open your email application; they do not submit information through this site. If you email hello@minnsace.com — for example, to ask for app support — Minnsace Inc. receives the address and information you choose to send, so we can respond to your enquiry. Please do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information.

Links to other services

Game links may take you to Steam or the App Store, separate services operated by Valve and Apple. Their own terms and privacy policies apply when you visit or use those services. This site does not embed Steam tracking, videos, or widgets.

Children

Our apps are suitable for general audiences. Because our apps collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Questions

For questions about this policy or our apps, contact Minnsace Inc. at hello@minnsace.com.
