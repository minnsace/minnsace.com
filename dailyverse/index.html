Daily Verse

A new Bible verse puzzle every day. Read the verse, then piece it back together phrase by phrase — a quiet minute in the Word, on your phone.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Daily Verse is free, with an optional one-time Plus unlock for the full puzzle archive. It collects no personal data — no account, no ads, no tracking. Choose your translation (World English Bible, KJV, ASV, or BSB) and your pace, and a fresh verse is waiting every morning.
